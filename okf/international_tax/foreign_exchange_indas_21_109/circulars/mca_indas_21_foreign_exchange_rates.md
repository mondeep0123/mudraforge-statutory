# Ind AS 21 & Ind AS 109: Foreign Exchange Accounting and Hedge Accounting

## 1. Scope and Classification of Foreign Currency Balances
Under Ind AS 21 (The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates), an entity must classify foreign currency denominated assets and liabilities into two distinct categories at each financial reporting date:
1. **Monetary Items**: Units of currency held and assets and liabilities to be received or paid in a fixed or determinable number of units of currency (e.g., trade receivables, trade payables, loans, cash and bank balances).
2. **Non-Monetary Items**: Items that do not carry a right to receive, or an obligation to deliver, a fixed or determinable number of units of currency (e.g., property, plant and equipment, intangible assets, inventories, prepaid expenses).

## 2. Reporting at Subsequent Reporting Dates (Ind AS 21.23)
- Foreign currency monetary items must be retranslated using the **closing spot exchange rate** at the balance sheet date.
- Non-monetary items that are measured in terms of historical cost in a foreign currency are translated using the exchange rate at the date of the transaction and are **never retranslated**.
- Non-monetary items measured at fair value in a foreign currency are translated using the exchange rates at the date when the fair value was determined.

## 3. Recognition of Exchange Differences (Ind AS 21.28)
Exchange differences arising on the settlement of monetary items or on translating monetary items at rates different from those at which they were translated on initial recognition during the period or in previous financial statements must be recognized in **Profit or Loss (P&L)** in the period in which they arise.

## 4. Cash Flow Hedge Accounting Exception (Ind AS 109.6.5)
Where an entity enters into derivative contracts (e.g., foreign exchange forwards) and designates them in a qualifying cash flow hedge of forecast foreign currency sales:
1. The **effective portion** of the gain or loss on the hedging instrument is recognized directly in **Other Comprehensive Income (OCI)** and accumulated under equity in the Cash Flow Hedge Reserve.
2. The **ineffective portion** is recognized immediately in **Profit or Loss**.
3. Upon realization of the forecasted underlying transaction, the accumulated reserve in OCI is reclassified to profit or loss.
