# CBDT Notification No. 86/2015: Rule 10CA Range Concept in Transfer Pricing

## 1. Executive Summary
Vide Notification No. 86/2015 dated 19 October 2015, the Central Board of Direct Taxes notified Rule 10CA and amended Rule 10B and Rule 10D, introducing the internationally recognized 'range concept' and the use of multiple-year data for determining the Arm's Length Price (ALP) in cross-border and specified domestic transactions.

## 2. Applicability of the Range Concept (Rule 10CA(1)-(3))
The range concept applies when the Most Appropriate Method (MAM) selected is:
1. Comparable Uncontrolled Price (CUP) method;
2. Resale Price Method (RPM);
3. Cost Plus Method (CPM); or
4. Transactional Net Margin Method (TNMM).

The range concept applies **strictly when the dataset contains six or more comparables** ($N \ge 6$).

## 3. Mathematical Percentile Construction (Rule 10CA(4)-(6))
The comparable prices or profit margins are arranged in ascending order.
1. **Thirty-Fifth (35th) Percentile**: Computed as $P_{35} = \text{Value at rank } [0.35 \times N]$. If $0.35 \times N$ is not a whole number, it is rounded up to the next integer. If it is a whole number, the average of the value at that rank and the immediately following rank is taken.
2. **Sixty-Fifth (65th) Percentile**: Computed as $P_{65} = \text{Value at rank } [0.65 \times N]$ using identical rounding conventions.
3. **Fiftieth (50th) Percentile (Median)**: Computed as $P_{50} = \text{Value at rank } [0.50 \times N]$.

## 4. Benchmarking and Adjustment Mechanics (Rule 10CA(7))
- **Within Range**: If the price or margin at which the international transaction actually occurred falls within the 35th to 65th percentile range, the actual transaction price is deemed to be at arm's length. No adjustment is made.
- **Outside Range**: If the actual transaction price falls outside the 35th–65th percentile range, the arm's length price is determined as the **median (50th percentile)** of the dataset. The difference between the actual transaction price and the median is added as a primary transfer pricing adjustment to taxable income.

## 5. Datasets with Less Than Six Comparables ($N < 6$)
Where the dataset contains fewer than 6 comparables:
1. The arithmetic mean of all values in the dataset is taken as the benchmark.
2. The statutory tolerance band applies (1% for wholesale distributors/traders where purchase cost $\ge 80\%$ of total costs; 3% for all other service providers and manufacturers).
3. If the variation between actual transaction price and the arithmetic mean does not exceed the tolerance band percentage, the actual price is accepted as ALP. Otherwise, the adjustment is made to the full arithmetic mean.

## 6. Secondary Adjustment Mandate (Section 92CE)
Where a primary transfer pricing adjustment exceeds ₹1 Crore (₹10,000,000):
1. The excess money must be repatriated into India from the Associated Enterprise within 90 days.
2. If not repatriated within 90 days, interest is imputed at one-year MCLR + 325 bps (for INR-denominated transactions) or 6-month SOFR + 300 bps (for foreign currency transactions).
3. Alternatively, the Indian company may elect to pay an additional tax of 18% (effective rate 20.9664% after 12% surcharge and 4% cess) under Section 92CE(2A).
