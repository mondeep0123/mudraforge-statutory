# CBDT Notification 03/2022: Electronic Filing of Form 10F

## 1. Statutory Context & Rule 21AB Requirements
Under Section 90(4) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, a non-resident assessee cannot claim relief under a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) unless they obtain a Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) from the government of the country of which they are a resident.

Where the TRC does not contain all particulars specified in Rule 21AB(1), Section 90(5) mandates the non-resident to furnish information in Form 10F.

## 2. Shift to Mandatory Electronic Portal Filing
By Notification No. 03/2022 dated 16th July 2022, the Directorate of Income Tax (Systems) mandated that Form 10F must be furnished electronically through the e-filing portal (incometax.gov.in) rather than as a manual self-declaration.

## 3. Mandatory Information Captured in Form 10F
1. Status of the assessee (Individual, Company, Firm, etc.).
2. Nationality or country of incorporation.
3. Assessee's Tax Identification Number (TIN) in the country of residence.
4. Period for which the residential status is applicable.
5. Address of the assessee in the country outside India during the period for which the certificate is applicable.

## 4. Operational Gate for Indian Remitters
An Indian corporate remitter applying treaty withholding rates under Section 195 cannot rely on manual paper Form 10F documents. The remitter must obtain the electronically verified Form 10F acknowledgment along with the certified TRC prior to executing the foreign remittance.
