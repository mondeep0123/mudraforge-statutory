# CBDT Circular No. 12 of 2022 — Guidelines under Section 194R

Under Section 194R of the Income-tax Act, 1961, any person providing a benefit or perquisite to a resident arising from business or profession must deduct 10% TDS if the aggregate value exceeds ₹20,000 in a financial year.

Key Principles:
1. Covers benefits in cash, in kind, or partly in cash and partly in kind.
2. Value is determined by fair market value (FMV) or purchase cost.
3. Dealer incentive trips, sponsorship gifts, and free samples above de minimis fall within Section 194R scope.
