# CBDT Circular No. 23 of 2017: Treatment of GST for Withholding Tax

## 1. Statutory Background
Following the introduction of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime with effect from 1st July 2017, representations were received by the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) requesting clarification on whether TDS under Chapter XVII-B of the Income-tax Act, 1961 should be deducted on the gross invoice amount inclusive of GST or strictly on the value of goods/services.

## 2. Definitive Statutory Rule
- Wherever in terms of the contract/agreement between the payer and the payee, the GST component (comprising CGST, SGST, IGST, or UTGST) on goods or services is **indicated separately in the tax invoice**, tax shall be deducted at source **strictly on the amount paid or credited without including such GST component**.
- Conversely, if the GST component is not separately stated and only a composite lump sum is billed, TDS must be deducted on the full invoice amount.
