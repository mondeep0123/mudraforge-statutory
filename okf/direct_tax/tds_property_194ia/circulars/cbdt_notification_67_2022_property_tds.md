# Central Board of Direct Taxes: Notification No. 67/2022 (Fourth Amendment Rules, 2022)

**Notification Reference**: Notification No. 67/2022 / F.No. 370142/19/2022-TPL  
**Issuing Authority**: Central Board of Direct Taxes, Ministry of Finance, Government of India  
**Date of Gazette Notification**: 21 June 2022 (effective 1 April 2022)  
**Governing Provisions**: Section 194-IA read with Section 295 of the Income-tax Act, 1961; Rules 30 and 31A; Form 26QB  

---

## 1. Statutory Amendment Context (Finance Act, 2022)

Prior to the Finance Act, 2022, tax deduction at source under Section 194-IA was computed strictly on the contractual consideration agreed between the transferee and transferor. Sub-section (1) and sub-section (2) of Section 194-IA were amended by the Finance Act, 2022 w.e.f. 1 April 2022 to align the tax withholding base with the anti-abuse provisions of Section 43CA and Section 50C.

Under the amended law:
1. **Taxable Withholding Base**:
   - The transferee is obligated to deduct 1.0% tax at source on the **sum of consideration or the Stamp Duty Value (SDV) of such property, whichever is higher**.
2. **De Minimis Threshold (Section 194-IA(2))**:
   - No deduction is made where **both** the consideration for the transfer of an immovable property **and** the stamp duty value of such property are less than INR 50,00,000.
   - If either the contractual consideration or the stamp duty value reaches or exceeds INR 50,00,000, Section 194-IA is triggered on the greater of the two figures.

---

## 2. Amendments to Income-tax Rules & Form 26QB

Notification No. 67/2022 amended Appendix II to the Income-tax Rules, 1962, substituting a comprehensive electronic Form 26QB (Challan-cum-statement of deduction of tax under Section 194-IA):

- **Separate Reporting of Consideration and Stamp Duty Value**: Form 26QB mandates separate line-item disclosure of the contractual sales consideration and the stamp duty valuation determined by the state stamp valuation authority (Sub-Registrar).
- **Incidental Transfer Charges Breakdown**: By virtue of the Explanation to Section 194-IA inserted by Finance Act, 2019, the contractual consideration must explicitly incorporate:
  - Club membership fees
  - Car parking allotment charges
  - Electricity, water, and power back-up facility charges
  - Advance maintenance charges
  - Sinking fund / corpus fund contributions
  - Any other charges incidental to the transfer of the immovable property.
- **Installment-Based Withholding**: Where payment is made in installments across construction milestones, TDS must be deducted on each installment. Where the total property SDV exceeds the total contractual consideration, the incremental tax is apportioned across remaining installments.

## 3. Compliance Timelines & Failure Consequences

| Requirement | Statutory Rule | Statutory Deadline | Penalty for Non-Compliance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Form 26QB Electronic Filing** | Rule 30(2C) & Rule 31A(4C) | Within 30 days from the end of the month in which deduction is made | Fee of INR 200/day under Section 234E; Penalty up to tax amount under Section 271H |
| **Form 16B Certificate Issuance** | Rule 31(1)(b) | Within 15 days from the due date for furnishing Form 26QB | Penalty of INR 500/day under Section 272A(2)(g) |
| **Missing / Inoperative PAN** | Section 206AA | At time of credit or payment | Mandatory higher rate of 20.0% TDS |
