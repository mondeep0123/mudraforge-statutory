# CBDT Circular No. 29/2019: Corporate Tax Regime Clarifications

## 1. Concessional Tax Regime under Section 115BAA
The Taxation Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019 introduced Section 115BAA into the Income-tax Act, 1961, providing domestic companies an irrevocable option to compute total income at a concessional rate of 22% (effective tax rate of 25.168% including 10% surcharge and 4% cess).

## 2. Inapplicability of Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT)
Sub-section (5A) of Section 115JB provides that the provisions of Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) under Section 115JB shall not apply to a person who has exercised the option referred to under Section 115BAA.

## 3. Treatment of Accumulated MAT Credit under Section 115JAA
Where a corporate assessee exercises the option to be governed by Section 115BAA:
1. The company cannot set off any brought forward MAT credit accumulated under Section 115JAA against tax payable under Section 115BAA.
2. The accumulated MAT credit balance is effectively forfeited once Form 10-IC is exercised.
3. Therefore, corporate finance departments must model whether exhausting available MAT credit under the Old Regime results in lower cash outflow before opting into Section 115BAA.

## 4. Mandatory Form 10-IC Filing Requirement
The option must be exercised in Form 10-IC electronically on or before the due date specified under sub-section (1) of Section 139 for furnishing the return of income for any previous year relevant to the assessment year. Once exercised, the option cannot be subsequently withdrawn for the same or any other previous year.
