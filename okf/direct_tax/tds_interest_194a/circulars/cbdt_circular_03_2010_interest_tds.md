# CBDT Circular No. 03 of 2010: Deduction of Tax at Source under Section 194A

## 1. Statutory Background
Section 194A of the Income-tax Act, 1961 mandates the deduction of income-tax at source from payment of any income by way of interest other than "Interest on securities" to a resident.

## 2. Applicability & Withholding Thresholds
Tax is deductible at the time of credit of such income to the account of the payee (including interest accrued and credited to "Interest payable account" or "Suspense account") or at the time of payment in cash or by issue of a cheque or draft or by any other mode, whichever is earlier.

* **Commercial Borrowings, Vendor Delayed Payments, and Inter-Corporate Deposits (ICDs)**: Threshold of ₹5,000 per financial year.
* **Banking Companies, Co-operative Banks, and Post Office Deposits**: Threshold of ₹40,000 per financial year (₹50,000 in case of a resident Senior Citizen).

## 3. Interaction with MSMED Act, 2006 (Section 16 & Section 23)
Where a buyer fails to make payment to a Micro or Small enterprise within the statutory period under Section 15 of the MSMED Act, 2006, penal compound interest with monthly rests is payable under Section 16 of the MSMED Act.
* Such payment falls within the definition of "interest" under Section 2(28A) of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
* If the cumulative interest in the financial year exceeds ₹5,000, tax must be withheld under Section 194A at 10% (or 20% in the absence of PAN under Section 206AA).
* **Mandatory Disallowance**: Under Section 23 of the MSMED Act, 2006, notwithstanding anything contained in the Income-tax Act, 1961, the amount of interest payable or paid by any buyer under Section 16 shall not be allowed as deduction in computing the taxable income of such buyer.
