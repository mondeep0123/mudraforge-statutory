# CBDT Circular No. 23 of 2017: Treatment of GST on Contractor Invoices

## 1. Statutory Clarification
Under Section 194C of the Income-tax Act, 1961, tax is deductible on the payment of any sum to a resident contractor. Where the contractor indicates the GST component (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST) separately on the tax invoice, tax shall be deducted at source strictly on the amount paid or credited without including such GST component.

## 2. Invoicing Standard
- Separately itemized GST: Deduct TDS at 1% or 2% on pre-tax basic amount.
- Composite billing without separate GST itemization: Deduct TDS on gross invoice amount.
