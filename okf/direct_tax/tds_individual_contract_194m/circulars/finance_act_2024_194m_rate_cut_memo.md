# Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 — Section 194M Rate Rationalization

## 1. Statutory Scope
Section 194M was introduced by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2019 to bring large contractual, commission, and professional payments made by individuals and HUFs (not covered by 194C, 194H, or 194J) exceeding INR 50 Lakhs in a financial year within the tax deduction at source net.

## 2. Rationalized Statutory Rate
Under the amendments introduced by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024:
- Pre-1 October 2024 Rate: 5.0%
- W.e.f. 1 October 2024 Rate: 2.0%

## 3. Compliance Mechanism
Deductors do not require a Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) and discharge withholding liability using Permanent Account Numbers (PANs) of deductor and payee through Form 26QD.
