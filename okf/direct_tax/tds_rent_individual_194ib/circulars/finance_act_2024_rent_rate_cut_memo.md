# Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 — Section 194-IB Rate Rationalization

## 1. Statutory Background
Section 194-IB of the Income-tax Act, 1961 requires individuals and HUFs (not covered under Section 194-I tax audit thresholds) paying rent exceeding INR 50,000 per month or part of a month to deduct TDS.

## 2. Rate Rationalization
To rationalize TDS rates and ease compliance burdens on individual tenants:
- Prior Rate (Pre-1 October 2024): 5.0%
- Amended Rate (W.e.f. 1 October 2024): 2.0%

## 3. Statutory Ceiling on Section 206AA
Per Section 194-IB(4), where Section 206AA applies due to failure by the lessor to furnish a valid PAN, the tax deducted shall not exceed the amount of rent payable for the last month of the previous year or tenancy.
