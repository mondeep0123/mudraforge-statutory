# Statutory Amendment: Section 194J Fees for Technical Services (FTS)

## 1. Background & Legislative Rationale
Prior to the Finance Act, 2020, both "Fees for Technical Services" (FTS) and "Fees for Professional Services" were subjected to withholding tax at the rate of 10% under Section 194J. However, payments to contractors under Section 194C were taxed at 1% / 2%, creating massive litigation where assessing officers attempted to reclassify contractor works as technical services to demand 10% TDS.

## 2. Statutory Rate Decoupling
To eliminate friction and harmonize the tax rates:
1. **Fees for Technical Services (FTS)** (other than professional services) under Section 194J(1)(a) are subjected to TDS at the reduced rate of **2%**.
2. **Royalty for Sale, Distribution, or Exhibition of Cinematographic Films**: Deductible at **2%**.
3. **Professional Fees**: Payments to lawyers, chartered accountants, doctors, architects, and notified professional bodies remain taxable at the standard rate of **10%**.
4. **Director Remuneration (Non-Salary)**: Taxable at **10%**.
