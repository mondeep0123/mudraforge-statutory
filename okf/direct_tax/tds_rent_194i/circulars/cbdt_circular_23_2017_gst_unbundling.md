# CBDT Circular No. 23 of 2017: Treatment of GST on Rent Invoices

## 1. Statutory Clarification
Under Section 194I of the Income-tax Act, 1961, tax is deductible on payment of rent exceeding ₹2,40,000 per financial year. Where the landlord/lessor indicates GST separately on the invoice or rent receipt, tax shall be deducted at source strictly on the basic rent amount without including the GST component.

## 2. Invoicing Standard
- Separately itemized GST: Deduct TDS at 2% (machinery) or 10% (land/building) on basic rental amount.
- Composite billing without separate GST itemization: Deduct TDS on gross billed amount.
