# CBDT Circular No. 20 of 2020: Guidelines under Section 194-O of the Income-tax Act, 1961

## 1. Statutory Objective
Section 194-O was inserted by the Finance Act, 2020 with effect from 1 October 2020 to bring digital marketplace sellers within the tax deduction net.

## 2. Applicability & Scope of Gross Amount
An e-commerce operator must deduct tax at source at the time of credit of amount of sale or service to the account of an e-commerce participant or at the time of payment thereof, whichever is earlier.
* Under the Explanation to Section 194-O(1), any payment made directly by a purchaser of goods or recipient of services to an e-commerce participant shall be deemed to be an amount credited or paid by the e-commerce operator.
* Tax must be deducted on the gross amount of sales or provision of services.

## 3. Statutory Rates & Threshold Exemption
* **Standard Rate**:
  * Prior to 1 October 2024: 1.0%
  * On or after 1 October 2024: 0.1% (Amended by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024)
* **Missing PAN**: Where the e-commerce participant does not furnish PAN/Aadhaar, tax is deductible at 5.0% under the second proviso to Section 206AA(1).
* **De Minimis Threshold**:
  * Individual / HUF: Exempt if gross turnover through the operator does not exceed ₹5,00,000 in the financial year and PAN is furnished.
  * Companies, LLPs, Firms: No threshold exemption; tax applies from the first rupee.

## 4. Statutory Precedence over Section 194Q and Section 206C(1H)
Under sub-section (3) of Section 194-O read with sub-section (5) of Section 194Q and Section 206C(1H):
* If tax is deducted or deductible under Section 194-O, no tax shall be deducted under Section 194Q by the buyer.
* Similarly, no tax shall be collected by the seller under Section 206C(1H).
* Section 194-O takes statutory precedence over all goods transaction withholding and collection provisions.
