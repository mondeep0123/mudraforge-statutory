# CBDT Circular No. 11 of 2021 — Compliance Check for Section 206AB

Under Section 206AB of the Income-tax Act, 1961, tax is deductible at higher rates on payments to specified persons who have defaulted on filing their income tax returns and have aggregate TDS/TCS of ₹50,000 or more.

Key Rules:
1. Deductor may use the API or portal functionality 'Compliance Check for Section 206AB & 206CCA'.
2. If vendor PAN is marked as a specified person, tax must be withheld at twice the normal rate or 5%, whichever is higher.
3. If vendor also lacks valid PAN (Section 206AA), higher of 206AA or 206AB applies.
