# Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024: Amendment to Section 194H

## 1. Statutory Amendment
Section 194H of the Income-tax Act, 1961 was amended by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (Act No. 15 of 2024) to reduce the rate of withholding tax on commission or brokerage:
- Prior to 1 October 2024: 5%
- On or after 1 October 2024: 2%

## 2. Invariable Conditions
- Threshold of ₹15,000 in aggregate per financial year remains unchanged.
- Failure to furnish PAN continues to attract 20% deduction under Section 206AA.
