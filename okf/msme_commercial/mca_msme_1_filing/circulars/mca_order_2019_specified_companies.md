# MCA Order S.O. 368(E) dated 22 January 2019: Half-Yearly Form MSME-1

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Intent
Under Section 405 of the Companies Act, 2013, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs notified the *Specified Companies (Furnishing of information about payment to micro and small enterprise suppliers) Order, 2019* on 22nd January 2019. 

The order mandates all "specified companies" to report amounts remaining unpaid to micro and small enterprises exceeding 45 days from the date of acceptance or deemed acceptance.

## 2. Definition of "Specified Company"
A "specified company" is defined as any company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 (or previous company law) that:
1. Gets supplies of goods or services from micro and small enterprises; and
2. Whose payments to such suppliers exceed forty-five (45) days from the date of acceptance or the date of deemed acceptance of the goods or services.

## 3. Mandatory Reporting Frequency & Statutory Due Dates
Form MSME-1 must be filed electronically on the MCA portal twice every financial year:
- **Half-Year 1 (April to September)**: Filing deadline is strictly **31st October**.
- **Half-Year 2 (October to March)**: Filing deadline is strictly **30th April**.

## 4. Key Mandatory Disclosures in e-Form MSME-1
1. Total amount outstanding to micro and small enterprises for more than 45 days as on the end of the half-year.
2. Line-item particulars of each defaulting transaction:
   - Name of supplier.
   - Permanent Account Number (PAN) of supplier.
   - Date from which the amount is due.
   - Exact amount due.
   - Specific, substantiated reason for delay in payment.

## 5. Penalties for Default
Under Section 405(4) of the Companies Act, 2013:
- The company is liable to a penalty of ₹20,000, and in case of continuing failure, a further penalty of ₹1,000 for each day after the first, subject to a maximum of ₹3,00,000.
- Every officer of the company who is in default is liable to a penalty of ₹20,000, with a continuing penalty of ₹1,000 per day subject to a maximum of ₹3,00,000.
