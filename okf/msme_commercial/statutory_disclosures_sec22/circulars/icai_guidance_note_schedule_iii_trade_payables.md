# ICAI Guidance Note: Trade Payables Aging & MSMED Statutory Reporting

## 1. Statutory Foundation & Reporting Framework
Pursuant to MCA Notification G.S.R. 207(E) dated 24th March 2021, Schedule III to the Companies Act, 2013 mandates an explicit Trade Payables Aging Schedule in the financial statements for financial years commencing on or after 1st April 2021.

This requirement operates alongside Section 22 of the MSMED Act, 2006, which prescribes five quantitative disclosures in the Notes to Accounts for every entity undergoing statutory audit.

## 2. Schedule III Aging Presentation Requirements
Trade Payables must be sub-classified into:
1. **MSME Dues - Undisputed**:
   - Outstanding for: Less than 1 year, 1–2 years, 2–3 years, More than 3 years.
2. **MSME Dues - Disputed**:
   - Outstanding for: Less than 1 year, 1–2 years, 2–3 years, More than 3 years.
3. **Others (Non-MSME) - Undisputed & Disputed**.
4. **Unbilled Dues**: Separately disclosed in the notes.

Where dues are outstanding from the date of the invoice (or date of payment due), the aging must strictly be calculated from the due date of payment or transaction date if no due date is specified.

## 3. The Five Mandatory Section 22 Notes to Accounts
Under Section 22 of the MSMED Act, 2006, the audited financial statements must disclose:
- *(a)* The principal amount and the interest due thereon (to be shown separately) remaining unpaid to any supplier as at the end of each accounting year;
- *(b)* The amount of interest paid by the buyer in terms of Section 16, along with the amount of the payment made to the supplier beyond the appointed day during each accounting year;
- *(c)* The amount of interest due and payable for the period of delay in making payment (which have been paid but beyond the appointed day during the year) but without adding the interest specified under this Act;
- *(d)* The amount of interest accrued and remaining unpaid at the end of each accounting year; and
- *(e)* The amount of further interest remaining due and payable even in the succeeding years, until such date when the interest dues as above are actually paid to the small enterprise.

## 4. Book vs. Tax Treatment: Section 23 Disallowance
Section 23 of the MSMED Act states: *"Notwithstanding anything contained in the Income-tax Act, 1961, the amount of interest payable or paid by any buyer, under or in accordance with the provisions of this Act, shall not, for the purposes of computation of income under Income-tax Act, be allowed as deduction."*

Accordingly, any compound interest computed under Section 16 and debited to the Profit & Loss Account must be added back in the computation of total income in the entity's income tax return (ITR-6).
