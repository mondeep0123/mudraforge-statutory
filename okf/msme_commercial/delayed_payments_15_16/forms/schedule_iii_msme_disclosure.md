## 2. Inadmissibility of Penal Interest under Income Tax
Under Section 23 of the MSMED Act, 2006, the compound penal interest paid or payable under Section 16 **is strictly not deductible as business expenditure** for computing taxable income under the Income-tax Act, 1961.
