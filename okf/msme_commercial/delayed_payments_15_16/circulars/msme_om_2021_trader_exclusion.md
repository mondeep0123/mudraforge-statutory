# Ministry of MSME Policy: Exclusion of Retail & Wholesale Traders

## 1. Background
By Office Memorandum No. 5/2(2)/2020-E/P&G/Policy dated 2nd July 2021, the Ministry of MSME allowed Retail and Wholesale Trade (National Industry Classification - NIC Codes 45, 46, and 47) to register on the Udyam Registration Portal.

## 2. Definitive Exclusion from Delayed Payment Provisions
Clarifying the scope of benefits available to traders, the Ministry of MSME and the Office of the Development Commissioner issued OM No. 1/4(1)/2021-P&G/Policy dated 1st September 2021, establishing the following statutory invariants:

1. **Priority Sector Lending (PSL) Only**:
   The benefits conferred on Retail and Wholesale Trade MSMEs are **strictly restricted to Priority Sector Lending (PSL)** from commercial banking institutions.
2. **Exclusion from Chapter V (Delayed Payments)**:
   Any other benefits under the MSMED Act, 2006—specifically including Chapter V provisions regarding:
   - Mandatory payment timelines within 45 days (Section 15)
   - Compound penal interest at 3 times the RBI Bank Rate (Section 16)
   - Access to State Micro & Small Enterprise Facilitation Councils (MSEFC)
   **are strictly excluded and unavailable to retail and wholesale traders**.
3. **Linkage to Income-tax Act Section 43B(h)**:
   Because the delayed payment clock of Section 15 of the MSMED Act does not apply to retail/wholesale traders, disallowance under Section 43B(h) of the Income-tax Act **cannot be applied** to overdue payables owed to vendors whose Udyam registration is exclusively classified under NIC Codes 45, 46, or 47 (Trading).
