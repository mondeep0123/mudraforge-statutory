# CBIC Circular No. 172/04/2022-GST: Clarification on Section 17(5)(b) Blocked Credits

## 1. Issue Clarified by CBIC
Whether the proviso following Section 17(5)(b)(iii) of the CGST Act—which states that Input Tax Credit (ITC) shall be available where it is *obligatory for an employer to provide the same to its employees under any law for the time being in force*—applies to all sub-clauses under Section 17(5)(b) or only to sub-clause (iii).

## 2. Definitive Statutory Clarification
1. **Universal Application of the Proviso**:
   The CBIC clarified that the proviso after sub-clause (iii) of clause (b) of Section 17(5) of the CGST Act **applies to the entirety of Section 17(5)(b)** (including sub-clauses (i), (ii), and (iii)).
2. **Eligible Categories Under Statutory Mandate**:
   - Food, beverages, and canteen facilities (e.g., obligatory canteen under Section 46 of the Factories Act, 1948 for factories employing > 250 workers).
   - Health insurance and life insurance (when mandated by statutory labour regulations or government notifications).
   - Employee transport / cab services (when mandated under state-specific Shop & Establishment Acts for night-shift female employees).
3. **Audit Evidence Requirement**:
   To claim ITC under this proviso, the registered person must maintain the statutory enactment citation or government notification establishing that the provision of goods/services was a compulsory legal obligation.
