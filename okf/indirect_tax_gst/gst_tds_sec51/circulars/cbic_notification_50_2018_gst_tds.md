# CBIC Notification No. 50/2018-Central Tax — TDS under GST

Under Section 51 of the CGST Act, 2017, government departments, local authorities, and specified PSUs must deduct 2% tax at source (1% CGST + 1% SGST or 2% IGST) on taxable contracts exceeding ₹2,50,000.

Key Rules:
1. Threshold of ₹2.5 Lakhs applies to total contract value, excluding taxes.
2. Deductions deposited in GSTR-7 by the 10th of subsequent month.
3. Certificate Form GSTR-7A made available to supplier on GST portal.
