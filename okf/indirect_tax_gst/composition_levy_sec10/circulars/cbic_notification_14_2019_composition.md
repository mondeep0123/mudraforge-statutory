# CBIC Notification No. 14/2019-Central Tax — Composition Scheme Thresholds

Under Section 10 of the CGST Act, 2017, the turnover threshold for composition scheme eligibility is ₹1.5 Crore (₹75 Lakhs for specified special category states).

Key Restraints:
1. Composition taxpayers must NOT collect tax on supplies.
2. Must issue a Bill of Supply, NOT a Tax Invoice.
3. Recipients are prohibited from taking ITC on composition purchases.
