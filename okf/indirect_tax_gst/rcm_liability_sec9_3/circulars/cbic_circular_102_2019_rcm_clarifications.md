# CBIC Circular No. 102/21/2019-GST: RCM Mechanics & Statutory Obligations

## 1. Statutory Mechanism of Reverse Charge
Under Section 9(3) of the CGST Act, 2017, the liability to pay GST is shifted from the supplier to the recipient for notified categories of goods and services specified under Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate).

## 2. Key Specified Services under Reverse Charge
1. **Goods Transport Agency (GTA)**: Services provided by a GTA in respect of transportation of goods by road to any factory, registered society, cooperative society, body corporate, or registered partnership firm (where GTA has not opted for forward charge @ 12%). Tax rate is 5% under RCM.
2. **Legal Services**: Services provided by an individual advocate, senior advocate, or firm of advocates by way of legal services to any business entity.
3. **Director Services**: Services provided by a director of a company or a body corporate to the said company or body corporate.
4. **Security Services**: Services provided by way of supply of security personnel provided to a registered person (excluding body corporate suppliers).

## 3. Strict Electronic Cash Ledger Payment Rule
Under Section 49(4) of the CGST Act, input tax credit in the Electronic Credit Ledger cannot be utilized for discharging output tax liability payable under reverse charge. 
All reverse charge tax liabilities must be discharged strictly in **cash** via the Electronic Cash Ledger in GSTR-3B (Table 3.1(d)).

## 4. Documentation: Self-Invoice and Payment Voucher
1. **Self-Invoice (Section 31(3)(f))**: Where supply is received from an unregistered person, the registered recipient must generate a self-invoice containing all prescribed particulars under Rule 46.
2. **Payment Voucher (Section 31(3)(g))**: At the time of making payment to the supplier, the recipient is legally required to issue a Payment Voucher containing particulars under Rule 52.

## 5. Seamless ITC Re-Claim
The tax discharged in cash under RCM becomes eligible as input tax credit in the hands of the recipient under Section 16(2) in the same tax period, reported under Table 4(A)(3) of GSTR-3B, subject to business use and Section 17(5) restrictions.
