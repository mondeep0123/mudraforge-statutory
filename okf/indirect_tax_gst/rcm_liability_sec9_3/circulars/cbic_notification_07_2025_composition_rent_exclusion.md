# CBIC Notification No. 07/2025-Central Tax (Rate) (Dated 16 January 2025)

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Background
Following the recommendations of the 55th GST Council meeting, the Central Government amended Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate) dated 28 June 2017 to rectify an unintended compliance burden placed on small taxpayers operating under the composition levy.

Earlier, Notification No. 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate) (effective 10 October 2024) inserted Entry 5AB, bringing the renting of any commercial immovable property (other than residential dwellings) by an unregistered supplier to *any registered person* under the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM).

Because Entry 5AB applied broadly to "any registered person", small enterprises registered under the Section 10 Composition Levy were forced to generate Self-Invoices (Section 31(3)(f)), issue Payment Vouchers (Section 31(3)(g)), and pay 18% GST in cash under GSTR-3B Table 3.1(d) without any entitlement to input tax credit (Section 10(4)).

## 2. Text of the Statutory Amendment
In Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate), in the Table, against serial number 5AB, in column (4), for the words **"any registered person"**, the words:
> **"any registered person, other than a person who has opted to pay tax under section 10"**

shall be substituted with effect from the **16th day of January, 2025**.

## 3. Legal Consequences for Accounts Payable (AP) Accounting
1. **Regular Registered Recipients (Section 9)**:
   - For regular GST-registered entities, Entry 5AB RCM remains fully active.
   - Rent paid to an unregistered landlord requires cash tax payment under Table 3.1(d) and credit availment under Table 4(A)(3).

2. **Composition Taxpayers (Section 10)**:
   - With effect from **16 January 2025**, composition taxpayers leasing commercial property from unregistered landlords are statutorily **EXCLUDED** from RCM liability.
   - The transaction falls out of reverse charge into forward charge / unregistered exempt territory.
   - Composition taxpayers have zero RCM payment liability and zero self-invoicing requirement under Entry 5AB.

3. **Temporal Application**:
   - *Prior to 10 October 2024*: No RCM on commercial rent by unregistered persons.
   - *10 October 2024 to 15 January 2025*: RCM attached to all registered recipients (both regular and composition).
   - *On or after 16 January 2025*: RCM attaches strictly to regular registered persons; composition taxpayers are excluded.
