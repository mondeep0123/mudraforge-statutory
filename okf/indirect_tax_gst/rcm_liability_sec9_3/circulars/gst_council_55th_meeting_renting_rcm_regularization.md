# 55th GST Council Meeting: Regularization of Commercial Renting RCM for Composition Taxpayers (21 December 2024)

## 1. Executive Context & Legislative History
At its 55th meeting held on 21 December 2024 at Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, under the chairpersonship of the Union Minister for Finance and Corporate Affairs, the GST Council addressed representations regarding the adverse impact of Notification No. 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate) dated 8 October 2024.

Notification No. 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate) inserted Entry 5AB in Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate), mandating reverse charge liability on renting of commercial immovable property by unregistered persons to *any registered person* effective 10 October 2024. This unintentionally imposed a non-creditable cash tax burden on small traders and manufacturers registered under the Section 10 composition scheme who could not claim input tax credit under Section 10(4).

## 2. Recommendation on Past Intervening Period (10 October 2024 to 15 January 2025)
To eliminate hardship and retrospective compliance disputes, the GST Council recommended:
1. **Prospective Amendment**: Amend Entry 5AB of Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate) to expressly exclude persons opting for the composition levy under Section 10 (effectuated via Notification No. 07/2025-Central Tax (Rate) w.e.f. 16 January 2025).
2. **"As Is Where Is" Basis Regularization**: For the intervening period from 10 October 2024 to 15 January 2025, past transactions are regularized on an *as is where is* basis:
   - **No Refund for Tax Already Paid**: If a composition taxpayer has already discharged GST under reverse charge mechanism in cash, no refund shall be granted. The discharged tax constitutes a permanent business expense.
   - **No Demand or Recovery for Unpaid Tax**: If a composition taxpayer has not discharged reverse charge tax on commercial rent during this intervening window, tax authorities shall not initiate retrospective recovery, demand notices, or penal proceedings.

## 3. Statutory Accounting & Audit Treatment
In statutory accounts payable reconciliation:
- Intervening bills where RCM was historically discharged: Classify as `RCM_COMPOSITION_INTERVENING_REGULARIZED_PAID`.
- Intervening bills where RCM was not discharged: Classify as `RCM_COMPOSITION_INTERVENING_REGULARIZED_UNPAID`, with zero cash tax liability and full consideration expensed to P&L.
- Indeterminate discharge status: Classify as `REQUIRES_HUMAN_VERIFICATION`.
