# CBIC Notification No. 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate): Reverse Charge on Commercial Property Renting

## 1. Statutory Background & Amendment
Exercising the powers conferred by Section 9(3) of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, the Central Government, on the recommendations of the GST Council (54th Meeting), notified **Notification No. 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate)** dated 8 October 2024, taking effect on **10 October 2024**.

This notification amends principal Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate) by inserting entry **5AB** into the Table of reverse charge services.

## 2. Scope of Reverse Charge Levy (Entry 5AB)
- **Category of Supply**: Service by way of renting of any property, other than residential dwelling, provided by any unregistered person to any registered person.
- **Supplier of Service**: Any person not registered under the CGST Act (unregistered landlord / property owner).
- **Recipient of Service**: Any person registered under the CGST Act (corporate entity, enterprise tenant, or registered business).
- **Tax Payment Liability**: The registered recipient is statutory liable to pay the GST (18%) under the **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)**.

## 3. Impact on Accounts Payable (AP) & Rent Disbursement
1. **Unregistered Landlord Leases**:
   Where a registered enterprise leases office spaces, retail outlets, warehouses, or commercial premises from an unregistered landlord:
   - The landlord will not charge GST on the rental bill.
   - The recipient enterprise MUST NOT treat the rental transaction as exempt or non-taxable.
   - The recipient must self-assess 18% GST under Section 9(3) and discharge it via cash in **GSTR-3B Table 3.1(d)**.
2. **Self-Invoicing & Payment Vouchers**:
   Under Section 31(3)(f), the recipient must generate a **Self-Invoice** for each rental accrual period. Under Section 31(3)(g), a **Payment Voucher** must be issued upon disbursing rental consideration to the landlord.
3. **Electronic Cash Ledger Discharge**:
   In accordance with Section 49(4) read with Section 9(3), RCM rental tax liability cannot be offset against balance in the Electronic Credit Ledger; it must be paid strictly in cash through the Electronic Cash Ledger.
4. **Input Tax Credit Claim**:
   Once discharged in cash, the tax paid under RCM is fully eligible for input tax credit under Section 16(2) in the same tax period, reported under **GSTR-3B Table 4(A)(3)**, provided the commercial property is used for furtherance of business.
