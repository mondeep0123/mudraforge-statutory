# CBIC Notification No. 10/2023-Central Tax — E-Invoicing Threshold

Mandatory electronic invoicing under Rule 48(4) of the CGST Rules, 2017 applies to registered persons whose aggregate turnover in any preceding financial year from 2017-18 exceeds ₹5 Crore.

Key Directives:
1. All B2B tax invoices, credit notes, and debit notes must carry a 64-character IRN and signed QR code.
2. Invoices issued without IRN are legally null and void under Rule 48(5).
3. Buyers receiving non-IRN invoices from > ₹5 Cr vendors cannot claim ITC under Section 16(2)(a).
