# CBIC Notification No. 19/2022-Central Tax: Amendment to Rule 37 of CGST Rules, 2017

## 1. Statutory Background
Under the second proviso to Section 16(2) of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, a registered person who avails input tax credit on any supply of goods or services is required to pay the supplier the value of supply along with tax within 180 days from the date of issue of invoice.

## 2. Substituted Rule 37 (Effective 1 October 2022)
Notification No. 19/2022-Central Tax substituted Rule 37(1) to state:
* Where a recipient fails to pay the supplier within 180 days, the recipient shall reverse an amount equal to the input tax credit proportionate to the unpaid amount.
* The reversal shall be furnished in Form GSTR-3B for the tax period immediately following the expiry of 180 days.
* Interest under Section 50(1) at the rate of 18% per annum is payable from the date of availing such credit till the date of payment/reversal.

## 3. Statutory Exemptions
The 180-day reversal condition does NOT apply to:
1. Supplies on which tax is payable under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) under Section 9(3) or Section 9(4).
2. Supplies deemed to have been made without consideration under Schedule I of the CGST Act.
3. Additions made to the value of supply on account of payments made by the recipient on behalf of the supplier under Section 15(2)(b).

## 4. Re-availment
Upon subsequent payment of the consideration to the supplier, the recipient can re-avail the reversed input tax credit in Form GSTR-3B. The statutory time restriction under Section 16(4) does not apply to re-availment of credit reversed under Rule 37.
