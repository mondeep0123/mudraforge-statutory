# CBIC Circular No. 192/04/2023-GST (Dated 17 July 2023)

## 1. Subject & Statutory Context
Clarification on charging of interest under section 50(3) of the CGST Act, 2017, in cases of wrong availment of Input Tax Credit (ITC) and reversal thereof under Rule 88B of the CGST Rules, 2017.

## 2. Issues Clarified by the Board

### Issue 1: Combined Ledger Balance vs Head-Wise Balance for IGST Credit
Where a registered person wrongly avails Input Tax Credit under the head of IGST and subsequently reverses it, the question arose whether interest under Section 50(3) read with Rule 88B(3) applies when the individual IGST ledger balance falls below the wrongly availed amount, but the combined balance of IGST, CGST, and SGST taken together remains sufficient.

**Clarification**:
In terms of Section 49(5) of the CGST Act, Input Tax Credit available under any head (IGST, CGST, or SGST) in the electronic credit ledger can be utilized for payment of IGST output liability. Therefore, to evaluate whether wrongly availed IGST credit has been utilized, the total balance of ITC across **all three major heads (IGST + CGST + SGST) taken together** must be considered.
- If the aggregate balance (IGST + CGST + SGST) never falls below the wrongly availed amount during the period from availment to reversal, **no interest under Section 50(3) is payable**.
- Interest attaches strictly to the extent of the shortfall in the combined balance and for the specific days of such dip.

### Issue 2: Cross-Utilization Restriction for CGST and SGST
- For wrongly availed **CGST credit**: CGST credit can be utilized for CGST and IGST liabilities, but cannot be utilized for SGST liability. Thus, the relevant available balance is `CGST + IGST`.
- For wrongly availed **SGST credit**: SGST credit can be utilized for SGST and IGST liabilities, but cannot be utilized for CGST liability. Thus, the relevant available balance is `SGST + IGST`.

### Issue 3: Strict Exclusion of Compensation Cess
Credit of Compensation Cess available in the electronic credit ledger cannot be taken into account when calculating the available balance for IGST, CGST, or SGST, because Cess credit can only be utilized for paying output Compensation Cess under Section 11 of the Goods and Services Tax (Compensation to States) Act, 2017.

## 3. Statutory Principles for Rule 37 Overdue Invoices
Where ITC is required to be reversed under Section 16(2) second proviso read with Rule 37(1) for unpaid supplier bills > 180 days, interest under Section 50 attaches only if the unreversed credit was utilized in terms of Rule 88B(3) as interpreted by Circular No. 192/04/2023-GST. If the relevant ledger balance remained at or above the unreversed ITC, the credit remained unutilized and interest exposure is ₹0.00.
