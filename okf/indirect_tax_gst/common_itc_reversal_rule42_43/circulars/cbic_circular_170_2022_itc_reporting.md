# CBIC Circular No. 170/02/2022-GST: Mandatory Reporting of Permanent Reversals under Rules 42 and 43 in Table 4(B)(1)

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Objective
Prior to July 2022, registered taxpayers frequently reported net Input Tax Credit (ITC) directly in Table 4(A) of Form GSTR-3B after netting off reversals under Rules 42 and 43. This obscured the audit trail between gross auto-drafted ITC in Form GSTR-2B and final credit availed. CBIC Circular No. 170/02/2022-GST established a mandatory, non-negotiable reporting framework enforcing the complete gross ingestion of ITC and strict disclosure of reversals.

## 2. Gross Availment & Permanent Segregation Architecture
Under the revised instructions:
1. **Gross Availment (Table 4(A))**: Registered persons must report all ITC auto-drafted in Form GSTR-2B into the relevant fields of Table 4(A), including ineligible credits.
2. **Permanent / Ineligible Reversals (Table 4(B)(1))**: All reversals which are absolute, definitive, and cannot be reclaimed under any circumstances MUST be reported exclusively in **Table 4(B)(1)**:
   - Reversal of common credit on inputs and input services attributable to exempt supplies ($D_1$) and non-business use ($D_2$) under Rule 42.
   - Monthly reversal of common credit on capital goods attributable to exempt supplies ($T_e$) under Rule 43.
   - Credit blocked under Section 17(5) (e.g. motor vehicles, works contract for immovable property, food and beverages).
   - Reversal by banking companies / financial institutions electing 50% credit reduction under Section 17(4) read with Rule 38.
3. **Prohibition of Netting-Off**: Netting off reversals against Table 4(A) credit is strictly prohibited. Table 4(C) Net ITC available must reflect:
   $$\text{Table 4(C)} = \text{Table 4(A)} - [\text{Table 4(B)(1)} + \text{Table 4(B)(2)}]$$

## 3. Mandatory Annual True-Up Synchronisation
The monthly reversals reported in Table 4(B)(1) represent provisional calculations. Pursuant to Rule 42(2), these monthly provisional reversals must be reconciled on an annual basis against the aggregate turnover for the entire financial year before the statutory cutoff date.
