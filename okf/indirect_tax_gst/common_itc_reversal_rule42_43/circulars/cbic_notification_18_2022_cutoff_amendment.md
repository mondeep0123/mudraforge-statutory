# CBIC Notification No. 18/2022-Central Tax: Extension of Annual True-Up and ITC Cutoff to 30th November

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Amendment
Pursuant to the amendments enacted by the Finance Act, 2022 (Act No. 6 of 2022), the Central Government appointed the 1st day of October, 2022 as the date on which provisions extending various compliance time limits came into force. CBIC Notification No. 18/2022-Central Tax notified these amendments across the Central Goods and Services Tax Rules, 2017.

## 2. Harmonisation of Annual True-Up Cutoff
Prior to this amendment, the annual true-up under Rule 42(2) was tied to the due date for furnishing the return for the month of September following the end of the financial year (typically 20th October).
Notification No. 18/2022-Central Tax harmonised this deadline with the amended Section 16(4) timeline:
1. **New Cutoff Date**: The annual reconciliation and true-up of common credit under Rule 42(2) must now be finalized and reported in Form GSTR-3B filed **on or before the 30th day of November** following the end of the financial year.
2. **Shortfall Reversal with Interest**:
   - Where the aggregate annual reversal ($D_1^{annual} + D_2^{annual}$) exceeds the sum of monthly reversals already made in Table 4(B)(1), the excess amount must be added to output tax liability or reversed in Table 4(B)(1) on or before 30th November.
   - Statutory interest under Section 50(1) at the rate of **18% per annum** is mandatorily payable on the shortfall, computed from the **1st day of April of the succeeding financial year** up to the date of actual payment/reversal.
3. **Excess Reversal Reclaim**:
   - Where the sum of monthly reversals exceeds the aggregate annual required reversal, the registered person is entitled to claim the excess amount back as credit in Form GSTR-3B filed on or before 30th November.
