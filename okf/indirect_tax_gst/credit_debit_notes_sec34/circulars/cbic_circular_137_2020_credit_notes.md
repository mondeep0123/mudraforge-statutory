# CBIC Circular No. 137/07/2020-GST — Credit Notes under Section 34

Section 34 of the CGST Act, 2017 governs the issuance and tax adjustments of credit and debit notes.

Key Clarifications:
1. Credit notes may be issued for deficiency in services, return of goods, or price adjustments post-invoice.
2. Deadline for declaring credit notes and adjusting output tax is the 30th day of November following the end of the financial year.
3. No output tax reduction if tax burden has been passed on to any other person (unjust enrichment doctrine).
