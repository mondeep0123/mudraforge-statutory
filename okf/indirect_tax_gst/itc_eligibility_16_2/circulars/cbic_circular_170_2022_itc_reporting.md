# CBIC Circular No. 170/02/2022-GST: Mandatory Reporting of ITC Reversals and Re-availment

## 1. Statutory Background
Prior to July 2022, taxpayers reported net Input Tax Credit (ITC) directly in Table 4(A) of GSTR-3B, without systematically disclosing temporary versus permanent reversals. CBIC Circular No. 170/02/2022-GST instituted a revised, non-negotiable reporting architecture to ensure complete audit trail reconciliation between auto-drafted GSTR-2B figures and final ITC availed.

## 2. Definitive Reporting Architecture in GSTR-3B Table 4

### A. Total Auto-Drafted Credit Ingestion (Table 4(A))
Taxpayers must ingest the entire gross ITC auto-populated from GSTR-2B into Table 4(A), including ineligible or temporarily restricted credits.

### B. Permanent Reversals (Table 4(B)(1))
Credits that are **permanently inadmissible** and can never be reclaimed must be reported in Table 4(B)(1):
- Blocked credits under Section 17(5) (motor vehicles, outdoor catering, employee gifts).
- Common credit reversals under Rule 42 (inputs/input services used for exempt supplies or non-business use).
- Capital goods credit reversals under Rule 43.

### C. Temporary Reversals (Table 4(B)(2))
Credits that are **temporarily reversed** but reclaimable upon future fulfillment of statutory conditions must be reported in Table 4(B)(2):
- **180-Day Rule Reversal (Section 16(2) Second Proviso & Rule 37)**: Where consideration along with tax is not paid to the supplier within 180 days from the invoice date.
- **Pending Receipt (Section 16(2)(b))**: Invoices auto-drafted in GSTR-2B where goods or services have not yet been actually received (e.g. goods in transit).
- **Section 16(2)(c) Vendor Default Contingency**: Invoices where tax has not been deposited by supplier.

### D. Re-availment of Reclaimed Credit (Table 4(A)(5) and Table 4(D)(1))
When the recipient subsequently pays the supplier the invoice amount along with tax:
1. The reclaimed credit is included in **Table 4(A)(5)** (All other ITC) in the month of payment.
2. The exact amount of reclaimed credit must be **separately disclosed in Table 4(D)(1)** (*ITC reclaimed which was reversed under Table 4(B)(2) in earlier tax period*).
3. **Statutory Time Limit Exemption**: The annual cutoff deadline under Section 16(4) does **NOT** apply to re-availment of credit under the third proviso to Section 16(2).

## 3. Statutory Exceptions to 180-Day Reversal Clock
Under Rule 37 and provisos to Section 16(2), the 180-day payment clock does not apply to:
1. **Reverse Charge Supplies**: Inward supplies subject to RCM under Section 9(3) or 9(4).
2. **Deemed Supplies Without Consideration**: Transactions between distinct persons or related parties under Schedule I.
3. **Value Additions under Section 15(2)(b)**: Supplier liabilities paid by recipient on behalf of supplier.
