# CBDT Notification No. 68/2021: Rule 11UAE Fair Market Value Determination

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Amendment
Prior to the Finance Act, 2021, the actual agreed monetary consideration in the Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) was typically treated as the full value of consideration for slump sales under Section 50B.

The Finance Act, 2021 inserted clause (ii) into sub-section (2) of Section 50B:
> *"the fair market value of the capital assets as on the date of transfer, calculated in the prescribed manner, shall be deemed to be the full value of the consideration received or accruing as a result of the transfer of such capital asset."*

CBDT subsequently notified **Rule 11UAE** of the Income-tax Rules, 1962, laying down the deterministic valuation formulas.

## 2. Deterministic Fair Market Value Formula
Under Rule 11UAE, the **Full Value of Consideration** deemed for capital gains computation is:

$$\text{Full Value of Consideration} = \max(\text{FMV}_1, \text{FMV}_2)$$

### A. FMV1 (Fair Market Value of Capital Assets Transferred)
$$\text{FMV}_1 = A + B + C + D - L$$

Where:
- **$A$**: Book value of all assets (other than jewelry, artistic work, shares, securities, and immovable property) as reduced by:
  - Advance income-tax paid
  - Unamortized deferred expenditure not representing any physical asset
- **$B$**: Fair market value of jewelry and artistic work based on registered valuer report
- **$C$**: Fair market value of shares and securities determined under Rule 11UA
- **$D$**: Stamp Duty Value (circle rate) adopted by government authority for transferred immovable property
- **$L$**: Book value of liabilities (excluding paid-up equity capital, reserves, provisions for unaccrued liabilities, and contingent liabilities)

### B. FMV2 (Fair Market Value of Consideration Received)
$$\text{FMV}_2 = E + F + G + H$$

Where:
- **$E$**: Monetary consideration received or accruing (cash, wire transfer, promissory notes)
- **$F$**: Fair market value of non-monetary consideration consisting of shares or securities determined under Rule 11UA
- **$G$**: Stamp duty value of immovable property received as consideration
- **$H$**: Fair market value of other non-monetary property received

## 3. Statutory Deductions: Net Worth Computation
Under Section 50B(2), the **Cost of Acquisition and Cost of Improvement** is statutorily defined as the aggregate **Net Worth** of the undertaking:

1. **Depreciable Assets**: Valued strictly at the **Written Down Value (WDV)** of the block of assets under Section 43(6)(c)(i)(C).
2. **Capital Assets with 100% Deductions (e.g. §35AD)**: Valued at **NIL**.
3. **Other Assets**: Valued at their book value (balance sheet value).
4. **Revaluation Invariant**: Any revaluation of assets (whether upward or downward) must be **completely ignored**.
5. **Less**: Book value of liabilities of that undertaking.

## 4. Disallowance of Indexation
Under Section 50B(2) second proviso:
> *"No indexation benefit under the second proviso to section 48 shall be available in respect of slump sale transfers."*
